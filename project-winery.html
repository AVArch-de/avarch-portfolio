Overview

Germany's first certified sustainable indoor swimming pool — a landmark in BIM-integrated public architecture.

This project is an indoor swimming hall in which I was involved in planning and implementation from Leistungsphase 1 through 5. It was a full BIM project in which model-based plans were produced. Collaboration and information exchange between architects, engineers, various specialist disciplines and the construction company was intensive and comprehensive.

The building stands as the first sustainability-certified indoor swimming pool in Germany, featuring a green roof and solar panels over the pool areas — a direct response to both ecological responsibility and the site's recurring flood risk, which required the structure to be elevated above the floodplain.

Project Highlights
Sustainability

First Certified Sustainable Pool in Germany

Green roof and solar panels integrated over the pool areas. The project set a national precedent for ecological certification in public aquatic facilities.

BIM

Full BIM Coordination

Model-based plans, visual model reviews, BIMx hypermodels, and Twinmotion visualisations. IFC-based quantity take-offs and cost estimation with NOVA AVA.

Holzbau

Timber Construction Detailing

Full development of timber construction details for the sports and pool areas, including concrete base connections and execution-level detail drawings.

Flood Protection

Elevated Construction

The building was raised above the floodplain level — a direct response to the district's recurring flood risk. Infrastructure resilience woven into the design from the outset.
