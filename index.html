About

Architect &
BIM Author

Based in Düsseldorf, I work at the intersection of architecture, BIM coordination, and sustainable construction. At POS4 Architekten, I lead BIM-integrated planning for public sports facilities and residential buildings — including Germany's first certified sustainable swimming pool.

My work spans design, technical execution, BIM coordination, and digital collaboration — with a commitment to timber construction and climate-conscious building methodologies.
