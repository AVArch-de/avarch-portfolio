Overview

A pioneering community hub in Bergisch Gladbach — integrating a 25-meter swimming facility with a high-performance sports hall in a sustainable timber structure.

Located in the Refrath district, this sport complex integrates a modern 25-meter swimming facility with a single-field sports hall. The project is defined by its commitment to sustainable materials — a load-bearing structure almost entirely of cross-laminated (CLT) and glued-laminated timber, with a warm larch-wood façade as the building's visible identity.

Planned and executed entirely using BIM, the project achieved a high degree of prefabrication — allowing the building shell to be erected in just a few weeks. The compact footprint preserves a valuable stand of old-growth trees on the site. A distinctive roof landscape seamlessly integrates the 1.5-meter height difference between the two halls.

Architectural Concept
🌳
Compact & Harmonious
The design uses a compact footprint to preserve a valuable stand of old-growth trees on the site. The layout responds directly to the existing natural context.
🏔
Dynamic Roof Landscape
A distinctive roof landscape seamlessly integrates the 1.5-meter height difference between the swimming and sports halls — creating a unified architectural silhouette.
☀
Fossil-Free Energy Concept
Entirely free of fossil fuels. Powered by a large-scale photovoltaic system on the green roof and a high-efficiency air-water heat pump.
♿
Fully Inclusive Design
Barrier-free accessibility implemented throughout — including tactile guidance systems for the visually impaired and specialised pool entry systems.
My BIM Contribution
Cross-Disciplinary BIM Coordination
Led BIM coordination across Architecture, TGA (building services), and Statics — managing model integration to achieve a collision-free construction process. Critical for the high degree of prefabrication required by the timber structure.
Timber & CLT Construction
Coordinated wall timber elements and the massive CLT structure. Directed all constructive joints within the model to facilitate seamless on-site assembly — allowing the shell to be erected in just a few weeks.
Façade & Technical Interfaces
Detailed execution of the sustainable larch-wood façade (Boden-Deckel-Schalung) and integrated curtain wall systems. Managed critical connection points: Sockel elements, door/window integrations, and roof consoles.
Recognition

BIM Champions
Award 2025

The project was nominated as a finalist for the BIM Champions Award 2025 in the Sustainability category. This recognition reflects the project's pioneering approach to digital planning — combining BIM-driven coordination, fossil-free energy systems, and sustainable timber construction to set a new benchmark for public sport facilities in Germany.
