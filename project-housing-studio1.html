Overview

A bold residential proposal for a corner site in Tetovo — defined by its striking red volume and subtractive massing strategy.

Developed during the first design studio of the Bachelor's programme at the State University of Tetovo. The brief called for a single-family house on a corner plot with two street frontages.

The design uses a subtractive massing strategy — beginning with a compact rectangular volume and carving out the garage, entrance, and terrace. The red façade creates a bold civic presence on the corner, while the grey service volume recedes. Drawings are in Albanian, reflecting the original academic context.
